# Driftwood Isle

Driftwood Isle is a cozy 3D island builder that runs in the browser on phones and desktops.

**Play:** https://19technologies.github.io/driftwood-isle/

## How to play

- Pick a piece from the tray, then tap the island to place it.
- Tap a house again to stack another floor, up to four. Houses come in six colours.
- Raise land from the sea, then add trees, flowers, paths and lamps.
- Your island earns **charm** as it grows. Charm unlocks new pieces: docks and boats at 15, a well at 35, a windmill at 60 and a lighthouse at 100.
- Drag to turn the island, and pinch or scroll to zoom.
- The buttons at the top undo your last change, switch between four times of day, and turn on a photo mode that hides the controls.

The island saves in your browser.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js and Google Fonts from the internet, so you need a connection.

## Built with

- [three.js](https://threejs.org) 0.149.0 from jsDelivr (MIT licence)
- Fredoka and Nunito from Google Fonts
- Wave sounds and chimes are synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API.
